Contact details

These are not editable here, and the reason is worth knowing. Your number and email appear 28 times across the site, and 20 of those are on the terms, privacy and refund pages. This editor only reaches the front page, so a box here would change the homepage and leave the policy pages quoting the old number. A site that disagrees with itself about how to reach you is worse than one that needs a developer for a change that happens once every few years.

To change them, ask your developer. It is a two-minute job done properly, in all 28 places at once.
